PowderRunbook is intentionally local and focused. It does not require an account, server connection or subscription.

Job or powder data

Jobs and powder lots are stored locally on the current device. Deleting the app may remove this local workspace, so keep important external records according to your own shop policy.

Cure and thickness values

PowderRunbook records values entered by the operator; it does not calculate or approve a cure schedule or certify a finished part. Check the powder manufacturer's current technical and safety data and your approved process.

Units

Open Settings to display temperature in °C or °F and coating thickness in µm or mil. Stored process values remain consistent when display units change.
