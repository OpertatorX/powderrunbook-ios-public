Privacy policy

Your shop stays your shop.

Effective October 5, 2026. PowderRunbook is designed to perform its core functions entirely on your device.

Data collection

PowderRunbook does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system or tracking technology.

Local records

Job records, powder-lot records and display preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to an OperatorX server.

Sharing

A job summary leaves the app only if you explicitly choose a destination from Apple's system share sheet.

Permissions

PowderRunbook does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access for its core functionality.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support page.
