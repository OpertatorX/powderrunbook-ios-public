Name: PowderRunbook
Subtitle: Powder Coating Job Log

Promotional text:
Track coating jobs, powder lots, cure records and QC notes in one focused offline runbook built for small powder-coating shops.

Description:
PowderRunbook is a focused offline job runbook for powder-coating shops and solo operators. Keep each part, powder lot, cure record and quality-control note together from intake to handoff—without turning your workshop into a full ERP project.

Built for the shop floor:
• move jobs through Intake, Prep, Mask, Coat, Cure, QC and Done;
• record customer, part, quantity and substrate details;
• link jobs to powder inventory lots and track recorded usage;
• record target and measured coating thickness;
• record cure target and observed temperature/time values;
• keep prep notes, job notes and operator-entered QC status;
• see active pipeline, recent completions, recorded powder usage and low-stock lots;
• search jobs by number, customer, part or powder;
• share a job summary through the iOS share sheet;
• switch display units between °C/°F and µm/mil.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no analytics, no subscription and no cloud upload. Your workshop records stay on your device unless you explicitly share a job summary.

PowderRunbook is a recordkeeping tool. It does not determine cure schedules, certify parts or replace manufacturer TDS/SDS, approved shop procedures, qualified operators or inspection equipment.
