# PowderRunbook App Privacy

App Store Connect privacy answers for version 1.0.0:

- Data collection: No data collected.
- Tracking: No.
- Third-party advertising: No.
- Analytics SDK: None.
- Account creation: None.
- Cloud sync: None.
- Location: Not requested.
- Camera / microphone / photos: Not requested.
- Contacts: Not requested.

Job records, powder-lot records and display preferences are stored locally on the device. Sharing occurs only when the user explicitly opens the iOS share sheet for a job summary.
